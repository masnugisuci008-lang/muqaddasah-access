01 · Braille tablet

Type a word. Feel it in braille.

Try the demo: the cells below show how your text would be raised as braille dots on the tablet.

Braille display

Demo uses standard Latin braille (Grade 1). Arabic and Quran braille are planned.

What we want it to do

  • Show text as raised braille dots you can read by touch
  • Speak every screen aloud for guidance
  • Run the full app, including the marketplace and lessons
  • Work offline for learning anywhere
  • Be affordable enough for real families
Goals, not final specsCo-designed with users
